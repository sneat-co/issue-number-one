## Recommendation and Priority

Do **not** commit to a broad relationship-aware issue-intelligence platform on
current evidence. Do not claim a graph moat, benchmark flywheel, representative
public opinion, employee-intelligence wedge, Moodometer-enhanced organisational
health, universal cross-product signal value or LinkedIn primacy.

Retain only this low-confidence, boundary-neutral learning thesis:

> A development manager or product manager may value a process that surfaces
> important pain points in time, turns them into a tested priority and provides
> an accountable response and visible terminal outcome.

This is a learning candidate, not a recommended product. The priority order is:

1. recruit one eligible development manager and one product manager and confirm
   the interview/evidence process;
2. complete the Accepted discovery cohort and apply its role-separated gates;
3. only after discovery success, request authority to test scarcity, visible
   action and one explicit context field independently;
4. treat any later early payment as facilitated-service evidence;
5. require founder-independent paid repeat before considering product transfer;
6. only then compare service, standalone and Sneat Work boundaries;
7. compare channels only after buyer, offer and product-transfer evidence are
   stable.

No-build remains live throughout. Public research, employee intelligence and
benchmarking are lower in the present **risk-and-learning order**, not disproved
on demand.

## Evidence and Confidence

- **Observed:** IssueNumber.one has a reachable landing and a waitlist write
  path; the page tests the older scarce-priority/`sneat.team` proposition. The
  authenticated application is still a scaffold, and the engagement inspected
  no demand, use, payment or retention result
  ([current-state baseline](research/current-state-and-demand-evidence.md)).
- **Sourced:** Organisations pay for adjacent listening, discovery, survey and
  research workflows. This establishes category spend, not an IssueNumber.one
  buyer, unmet wedge, price or willingness to pay
  ([customer/market evidence](research/customer-market-evidence.md)).
- **Sourced:** Public participation and internal employee intelligence differ
  in legitimacy, sampling, power, confidentiality, consent, rights, action loops
  and harm. Shared issue/vote vocabulary does not establish a shared product or
  trust promise ([product/trust evidence](research/product-modes-and-trust.md)).
- **Inferred:** Explicit relationship context may help bounded routing,
  coverage or accountability, but no graph-versus-non-graph result, learning
  curve, switching cost or network effect exists. Shared Sneat platform
  infrastructure is not customer value or defensibility by itself
  ([graph evidence](research/graph-value-and-defensibility.md)).
- **Sourced:** LinkedIn can target and measure professional contact; it has no
  evidenced buyer, qualified-account cost, paid conversion or retained revenue
  for this proposition ([growth evidence](research/growth-and-commercial-evidence.md)).
- **Resolution:** Independent challenge removed the Sneat Work-first claim,
  made no-build operational, separated scarcity from action, added paired trust
  roles, fenced service evidence from product evidence, narrowed the channel
  claim and labelled thresholds exploratory
  ([decision record](decisions/recommendation-resolution.md)).

**Confidence is low** on the strategic winner. Confidence is medium-high that
the broad platform and its trust/graph claims are unsupported now. The sponsor
confirmed that no aggregate product-evidence inventory is available, so
“traction unknown” remains the result; it does not become zero or positive
demand. The sponsor's direct management experience raises problem plausibility
but cannot establish recurrence, buyer, budget, payment or repeat. The decisive
unknown is whether independent development and product managers describe the
same recent, repeated and consequential failure with an inadequate current
alternative and named economic buyer/budget path.

## Alternatives and Dissent

- **Status quo/no-build:** May be the best business decision if the bounded
  discovery envelope fails or remains inconclusive; park the thesis for 90 days
  rather than broadening it.
- **Service, standalone and Sneat Work:** Observationally equivalent today.
  Sneat Work convenience is not buyer value, and no boundary is selected.
- **Mechanism:** Accountable action may explain all future value while scarce
  voting and relationship context add nothing or cause harm.
- **Other markets:** Employee intelligence and public participation/research
  carry higher trust or method burden; neither is rejected on relative demand
  because neither received symmetric discovery.
- **Commercial interpretation:** Early payment can prove founder-led
  facilitation or consulting, not product willingness to pay or a scalable
  commercial unit.
- **Channel interpretation:** An unmatched result establishes route feasibility,
  not a winner. The drafted comparison covers one organic, human-reviewed
  LinkedIn contact mechanism; paid and content LinkedIn remain unknown.
- **Scope containment:** A safe bounded cycle would provide no evidence for
  public research, organisational health, Moodometer integration, benchmarking
  or graph compounding. Those claims do not reopen automatically.

These are the retained Devil's Advocate positions, not editorial caveats
([full independent review](recommendations/devils-advocate-review.md#retained-dissent)).

## Risks and Dependencies

- **Buyer risk:** Development managers and product managers are candidates, not
  validated buyers; founder experience and People/HR spend do not transfer.
- **Trust risk:** Keep anonymous-author mappings, individual Moodometer signals,
  employee free text, individual votes, moderation/safeguarding cases, public
  research microdata, benchmark contributions and derived opinion/health/
  performance labels within their originating trust domains. Do not make an
  absolute anonymity promise while a linkable identity record remains.
- **Routing risk:** Do not auto-route through a manager or relationship that may
  be the subject of a concern; explicit context must be necessary, verified and
  compared with a manual field.
- **Method risk:** Open participation is not representative research. More
  participants do not repair incompatible instruments, selection bias or unsafe
  small/overlapping cohorts.
- **Commercial risk:** Founder warmth, facilitation and procurement ambiguity
  can create false positives; retain the complete eligible frame, refusals,
  exclusions, relationship strength, marginal delivery cost and exact payment
  state.
- **Dependency:** Discovery has approved outreach, confidentiality, raw-note
  storage, retention, withdrawal and no-incentive rules. It still needs eligible
  participant access. Any paid or later external motion needs separate sponsor
  authority.
