# IssuerNumber.One Strategy Engagement Iteration 003 Checkpoint

This checkpoint is a compact navigation aid. Its linked evidence and decisions
remain authoritative for their claims; this summary does not replace them.

## What we learned

- The broad relationship-aware issue-intelligence platform is not supportable
  as the next strategic commitment. No buyer, repeated use, payment, graph lift,
  benchmark flywheel, switching cost or channel economics was supplied. See the
  [executive synthesis](../executive-summary.md).
- A bounded priority-to-action job is only a reversible learning candidate. A
  facilitated service, standalone workflow, Sneat Work capability and no-build
  remain unresolved on current evidence. See the
  [resolution decision](../decisions/recommendation-resolution.md).
- Public participation/research and internal employee intelligence have
  different legitimacy, method, power and trust conditions. Their lower current
  ranking is risk-and-learning ordering, not evidence of weaker demand.
- The action loop, scarcity and explicit relationship context are separate
  mechanisms. Early payment can validate facilitation; explicit-context lift is
  not graph lift; unmatched outreach results show route feasibility, not a
  channel winner.

## What changed

- Strategic options, business, product and go-to-market recommendations and four
  experiment contracts were completed and moved to In Review.
- The independent Devil's Advocate recorded 14 challenges, including four
  blockers. The Managing Partner removed the Sneat Work-first claim, revised the
  experiment contracts and recorded every disposition in one decision.
- The independent Executive Editor reconciled sponsor asks, evidence,
  confidence, dissent, experiments and deferrals in `executive-summary.md`.
- No outreach, participant research, private-data access, campaign, product
  build, roadmap, architecture, canonical promotion or external change occurred.

## Decisions

- **Recommendation boundary:** Reject a broad platform commitment on current
  evidence; retain only the boundary-neutral learning candidate and no-build
  control.
- **Evidence sequence:** Existing aggregate evidence precedes participant
  contact; discovery precedes mechanism testing; service payment precedes a
  separate product-transfer gate; channel comparison follows stable paid repeat.
- **Trust containment:** Public research, employee intelligence, benchmarking,
  Moodometer integration and graph reuse require separate evidence and do not
  reopen automatically after a bounded success.
- **No promotion or execution:** All conclusions and contracts remain In Review
  and require sponsor decisions; none is canon or operational authority.

## Assumption changes

- Retire the assumption that ecosystem fit selects a Sneat Work boundary.
- Treat no-build as an operated strategic control, not a passive fallback.
- Treat facilitated payment as service evidence until founder-independent paid
  repeat covers full marginal delivery/support cost.
- Treat numeric experiment gates as exploratory and subject to one-case
  sensitivity, missing-data and withdrawal rules.
