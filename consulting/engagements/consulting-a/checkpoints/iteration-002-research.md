# IssuerNumber.One Strategy Engagement Iteration 002 Checkpoint

This checkpoint is a compact navigation aid. Its linked evidence and decisions
remain authoritative for their claims; this summary does not replace them.

## What we learned

- IssueNumber.one has a live demand-probe landing and waitlist write path, but
  the engagement has no traffic, signup, conversion, qualified-buyer, repeated-
  use, payment or revenue result. The page tests the older scarce-priority and
  `sneat.team` proposition rather than the newer relationship-aware framing.
  See the [current-state baseline](../research/current-state-and-demand-evidence.md).
- Organisations already pay for adjacent employee-listening, product-discovery,
  survey and research workflows. That establishes category spend, not an
  IssueNumber.one buyer, urgent switch trigger or scarce-vote wedge. Public
  participation and internal employee intelligence remain materially different
  jobs. See [customer and market evidence](../research/customer-market-evidence.md).
- Public research and internal organisation intelligence differ in legitimacy,
  power, sampling, confidentiality, consent, data rights, action loops and harm.
  Shared issue/vote vocabulary does not establish a shared trust proposition.
  See [product modes and trust](../research/product-modes-and-trust.md).
- Relationship context has plausible bounded value for routing, coverage,
  protected cohort interpretation and action ownership, but no graph-versus-
  non-graph outcome, learning curve, switching cost or network effect is
  evidenced. Shared infrastructure, distribution loops and network effects must
  remain separate claims. See [graph value and defensibility](../research/graph-value-and-defensibility.md).
- LinkedIn is instrumentable but not economically validated. The engagement has
  no buyer, qualified-account cost, conversion, sales-cycle or retained-revenue
  evidence that makes it primary over direct sales, partners, communities,
  content, invitations or no launch. See [growth and commercial evidence](../research/growth-and-commercial-evidence.md).

## What changed

- Five source-linked research reports were completed, reviewed by the Managing

- The approved specialist routing was executed: `gpt-5.6-terra`/high for market

- A graph-defensibility follow-up produced a useful evidence audit but no file

  the contracted artifact from the audited sources. No framework or canonical
  source was changed.
- Research stayed inside the approved public/repository boundary. No raw
  waitlist, analytics, personal data, private conversation, outreach or
  experiment was accessed or performed.

## Decisions

- **Research gate completed:** Tasks 1–5 in the accepted work plan have
  contract-complete artifacts. No recommendation is implied by completing the
  gate.
- **Safe evidence default retained:** Until the sponsor supplies an authorised
  non-identifying aggregate, traction, willingness to pay and repeat use remain
  unknown rather than zero or positive.
- **No promotion:** Findings remain In Review inside `consulting-a`; no product,
  relationship-graph, Sneat Work or Moodometer source is changed.
- **H6 universal form rejected:** It is not true on present evidence that every
  capability should create a reusable signal for another Sneat product. Some
  signal classes have stronger evidence for isolation than reuse.

## Assumption changes

- Retire the assumption that an existing shared Sneat platform graph makes every
  issue-derived signal useful or safe to reuse. Each use now requires its own
  beneficiary, necessity, permission, comprehension, outcome and harm case.
- Retire the assumption that more participants monotonically increase benchmark
  value. Comparability, recruitment, bias and disclosure determine the sign of
  added participation.
- Narrow “LinkedIn fit” to audience reach and measurement capability. Economic
  priority remains unknown until compared on qualified accounts through retained
  revenue and fully loaded cost.
- Preserve the assumption that the mandate's `IssuerNumber.One` names the
  current `IssueNumber.one` repository until the sponsor resolves naming.

## Open questions

- **Highest leverage, non-blocking:** Will the sponsor supply non-identifying
  monthly waitlist totals, aggregate source/plan counts, traffic/conversion if
  available, de-identified prior problem-conversation summaries and aggregate
  repeat-use, purchase-intent or budget-owner evidence? This changes the
  confidence and possibly the option ordering; absent it, synthesis uses
  “traction unknown.”
- Which candidate job has a recent, repeated, consequential decision and a
  named economic buyer? Public sources cannot resolve it.
- Is public mode intended as open participation, governed consultation,
  population research or separate offerings? The answer changes method,
  claims, buyer and trust.
- Which single relationship context changes a decision enough to justify its
  collection and harm surface over the current non-graph baseline?
