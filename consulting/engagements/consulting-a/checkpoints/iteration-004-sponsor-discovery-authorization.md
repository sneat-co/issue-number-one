# IssuerNumber.One Strategy Engagement Iteration 004 Checkpoint

This checkpoint is a compact navigation aid. Its linked evidence and decisions
remain authoritative for their claims; this summary does not replace them.

## What we learned

- No existing website, waitlist, usage, payment or retention evidence is
  available. Traction remains unknown.
- The sponsor's experience as a development manager and across corporations
  supplies a sharper founder hypothesis: material pain points often fail to
  bubble up in time to affect priorities and action. This is plausibility
  evidence, not independent customer validation.
- The sponsor accepted the learning direction after narrowing the primary
  segment from generic team leaders to development managers and product
  managers.

## What changed

- The [sponsor decision](../decisions/sponsor-discovery-authorization.md)
  accepted the bounded buyer/problem discovery and its authority limits.
- The [discovery contract](../experiments/buyer-problem-discovery.md) is now
  Accepted, with 4 development managers and 4 product managers as primary
  participants and 4 contextual interviews retained.
- A behavioural invitation, eligibility screen, interview guide, de-identified
  evidence matrix, role-stratified thresholds and execution safeguards are now
  explicit.
- No outreach has started; no later experiment, product, spend, architecture or
  promotion was authorised.

## Decisions

- **Evidence inventory:** Unavailable; do not infer zero or positive demand.
- **Learning thesis:** Accepted with development/product-manager amendment.
- **Discovery authority:** Up to 20–25 combined founder/research hours, no
  incentives, private raw notes outside Git, de-identified engagement evidence
  and raw-note deletion within 30 days of synthesis.
- **Boundary:** The decision does not select a product, service, standalone or
  Sneat Work implementation.

  the first primary cohort.

  non-warm recruitment explicitly.
- Report development-manager and product-manager evidence separately before any
  combined conclusion.

## Open questions

- Which eligible development and product managers can the sponsor introduce or

- Can the complete reachable frame support at least two non-warm cases, or will

## Next iteration

- **Objective:** Build the eligible frame, recruit the accepted cohort and
  execute behavioural interviews without presenting the product first.
- **Highest-leverage unknown:** Whether independent development and product
  managers describe the same recent, recurring and consequential pain-point-
  surfacing failure with a named buyer/budget path.
- **Smallest safe learning action:** Invite one eligible development manager and
  one eligible product manager using the accepted script, then confirm the
  screening and evidence process before expanding recruitment.
- **Owner and review trigger:** Sponsor for introductions/access; Managing
  Partner for screening, evidence and protocol. Review after the first two
  interviews, any safety deviation or the completed cohort.
