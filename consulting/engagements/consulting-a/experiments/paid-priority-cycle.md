# IssueNumber.one Paid Concierge Priority-to-Action Experiment

## Hypothesis

After buyer/problem and mechanism gates pass, a leader will pay for a manually
delivered bounded priority-to-action cycle, use the result in a real decision,
provide visible dispositions to contributors and pay to repeat or expand it.

## Assumptions

- A manual facilitated cycle can deliver the promised outcome without a product
  build.
- The operational decision can avoid employee-sensitive, safeguarding,
  performance or health content in this first test.
- The sponsor predeclares one fixed price no lower than the fully loaded marginal
  delivery cost, excluding research-learning overhead.
- Three organisations can test payment/action/repeat behaviour, not estimate a
  conversion rate or market size.

## Smallest Possible Implementation

Offer the same fixed-scope paid concierge cycle to three qualifying
organisations from the successful discovery cohort:

1. define one decision, owner, participants, close date and trust promise;
2. collect a bounded number of issues and scarce support through the smallest
   safe manual tool/process;
3. facilitate the decision owner response;
4. publish safe acknowledgement/action/defer/reject dispositions;
5. close and review the actual decision and contributor-visible outcome;
6. offer the same next cycle or an explicitly scoped expansion at the same
   commercial principle.

Do not build product software, import a broad relationship graph or collect
anonymous employee concerns. Existing tools and manual facilitation are the
correct implementation because payment and outcome—not UI use—are the question.

## Owner, Cost and Duration

- **Owner:** Sponsor/founder as commercial owner; named facilitator/researcher
  for protocol and evidence.
- **Participants:** Three authorised organisations, each with a decision owner
  and bounded contributor cohort.
- **Cost / budget source:** Sponsor preapproves delivery time and any tools; each
  organisation pays the predeclared non-token price. No budget is approved.
- **Duration:** Up to eight weeks across sale, three cycles and repeat offers.
- **Stop conditions:** Stop any cycle for sensitive employee/safeguarding data,
  unclear decision authority, false anonymity expectations, coercive
  participation, unpaid scope expansion or inability to provide terminal state.

## Metrics and Guardrails

- **Success:** At least 2 of 3 organisations pay or sign an enforceable paid
  commitment before delivery; at least 2 complete the cycle and document that
  it changed or confirmed a real decision; all material top issues receive a
  safe disposition; and at least 1 organisation pays for a repeat/expanded cycle
  within 30 days of close.
- **Failure:** No organisation pays; or paid cycles do not change/confirm a
  documented decision; or the owner cannot provide safe visible dispositions;
  or no repeat offer is accepted for value reasons.
- **Inconclusive:** One organisation pays and completes; procurement prevents a
  clean prepayment test despite named budget; or one cycle is invalidated by a
  facilitator/process deviation rather than the value proposition.
- **Guardrails / ethics:** Operational/non-sensitive scope only; explicit
  participation and attribution promise; no hidden worker monitoring; separate
  safeguarding route; de-identified engagement evidence; customer controls its
  operational records; no fabricated case study/publication; sponsor-approved
  contracts and retention.

## Expected Learning

This experiment tests buyer, payment, real decision value, other-actor action,
repeat use and delivery cost. It can also reveal whether value comes from
scarcity, facilitation, visible action, relationship context or ordinary process
discipline.

## Precommitted Decisions

- **On success:** Consider the smallest product implementation and make the
  standalone-versus-Sneat Work boundary decision using observed entry, context
  and repeat behaviour; do not add public/benchmark modes.
- **On failure:** Stop O2 and return to O0/O1 or the strongest newly evidenced
  alternative; do not broaden the platform narrative.
- **If inconclusive:** Run at most one additional comparable paid cycle only if
  the ambiguity is procurement or execution-specific and the protocol change is
  predeclared.
