# IssueNumber.one Relationship, Scarcity and Trust Lift Experiment

## Hypothesis

For one defined internal priority decision, a scarce issue/support mechanism
with visible disposition improves decision quality or time over the current
manual/non-graph workflow, and one verified responsibility relationship further
improves correct routing without reducing trust comprehension or increasing
identity inference.

## Assumptions

- A synthetic operational scenario can have a defensible decision/routing
  rubric without using real employee issues.
- Relevant decision users can distinguish a meaningful workflow difference in a
  small crossover pilot.
- Relationship context is tested one field at a time; an entire graph cannot be
  treated as one experimental variable.

## Smallest Possible Implementation

Create one synthetic cross-functional decision with the same issue evidence in
a counterbalanced two-by-two factorial:

- **A — neither:** ordinary issue input, no scarcity and no forced disposition;
- **B — scarcity only:** bounded issues/support, no forced disposition;
- **C — action only:** ordinary input plus acknowledgement/disposition/terminal
  response;
- **D — scarcity and action:** both mechanisms.

Only after the winning non-relationship mechanism is known, compare it with:

- **E — explicit context:** the same winning variant plus one verified
  accountable-unit or workflow-owner relationship necessary for a genuinely
  ambiguous route.

Run a randomised-order, counterbalanced pilot with 12 paired contributor/
decision-owner roles (24 participants). Contributors predict recipients,
identity exposure, permitted reuse, null action and terminal outcome, then see
the other actor's disposition. Owners choose the priority/action, route it and
explain limitations. Record decision-rubric score, routing accuracy, time,
unsafe identity guesses, trust comprehension and whether every material item
receives a contributor-visible disposition.

Before participant contact, an independent reviewer must certify the scenario,
rubric, fixed number of ambiguous cases, wording neutrality and that B/E add
only the declared variable. This is smaller than a product build and can
separate scarcity, action and explicit context; a bundled mock cannot.

## Owner, Cost and Duration

- **Owner:** Managing Partner or named product researcher; sponsor authorises
  participants.
- **Participants:** 12 paired contributors and decision owners matching the
  successful discovery job; no employee reporting a real sensitive concern.
- **Cost / budget source:** Approximately 25–30 research hours plus optional
  sponsor-approved incentives; no budget approved.
- **Duration:** Two weeks after the discovery success gate.
- **Stop conditions:** Stop for real confidential data, participant distress,
  inability to define a valid decision rubric, or evidence that the scenario
  embeds the desired answer.

## Metrics and Guardrails

- **Mechanism success (exploratory):** A scarcity effect requires B over A and/or
  D over C to improve median time by at least 20% without lowering rubric score.
  An action effect requires C over A and/or D over B to improve decision-rubric
  score or contributor-visible closure for at least 4 paired cases with no more
  than 1 regression. Report interaction rather than assigning bundled lift.
- **Explicit-context success (exploratory):** E changes the ambiguous route to
  the rubric-correct owner for at least 4 paired owners, none regress, decision
  score does not fall and zero additional unsafe identity/reuse guesses occur.
  Label any success `explicit-context lift`, never graph lift.
- **Trust/journey gate:** At least 10 of 12 contributor/owner pairs correctly
  predict identity/content recipients, reuse, null action and terminal state;
  every material item receives a contributor-visible disposition; and no
  variant creates an unsafe identity guess.
- **Failure:** No arm produces a decision/action improvement; scarcity adds no
  lift or suppresses material evidence; explicit context produces no correct
  routing lift; or any variant increases unsafe identity/reuse beliefs.
- **Inconclusive:** Time improvement is 10–19%; decision and speed conflict;
  fewer than 10 pairs understand the promise for correctable wording reasons;
  the ambiguity is invalid; or one pair's classification/withdrawal/missing data
  flips the branch. Exclude incomplete pairs from every paired comparison; fewer
  than 10 complete pairs is inconclusive.
- **Guardrails / ethics:** Synthetic non-sensitive content only; randomise order;
  predeclare rubric and independent scenario review before sessions; no concept-
  preference success metric; no claim of statistical population inference;
  capture de-identified results; sponsor approval before contact. Numeric
  cutoffs are exploratory decision gates chosen to require a visible multi-pair
  effect, not calibrated estimates; publish one-case sensitivity with every
  result.

## Expected Learning

The experiment distinguishes four possibilities: scarcity helps; visible action
helps; relationship context adds incremental routing value; or the ordinary
workflow is sufficient. It also tests whether the trust promise survives the
concept before any implementation.

## Precommitted Decisions

- **On success:** Preserve only the mechanisms that pass and allow the paid
  concierge cycle to test them in a real bounded decision.
- **On failure:** Remove failed scarcity or explicit-context claims from the paid
  offer; if the action loop alone adds no value, stop the priority-to-action
  direction.
- **If inconclusive:** Correct only the identified wording/rubric problem and
  repeat once; do not build to manufacture stronger evidence.
