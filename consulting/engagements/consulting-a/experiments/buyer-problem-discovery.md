# IssueNumber.one Buyer and Problem Discovery Experiment

## Hypothesis

Among development managers and product managers accountable for product and
delivery decisions, important pain points repeatedly fail to surface early or
clearly enough to affect priorities and action, creating a consequential
priority-to-action failure with a named economic buyer and budget path. This
contract does not test whether that job has stronger demand than employee
intelligence or public research.

## Assumptions

- A candidate can discuss a recent decision without sharing confidential,
  employee-sensitive or personal data.
- Recent behaviour is more reliable than feature preference or reactions to the
  IssueNumber.one concept.
- The sponsor's management experience is a useful source of language and
  scenarios, but cannot count as an interview, independent case or success.
- The sponsor has authorised lawful, respectful contact and a bounded evidence-
  retention process.
- Eight primary interviews can detect only a strong repeated pattern; four
  contextual interviews cannot order segments, estimate prevalence or reject a
  comparator market.

## Smallest Possible Implementation

The sponsor recorded the existing product-evidence inventory as unavailable.
Traction therefore remains unknown. The sponsor's firsthand management
experience is recorded separately as founder evidence and does not enter the
experiment numerator.

Conduct 12 structured, consented interviews:

- 4 development managers and 4 product managers who personally owned a recent
  case in which a material pain point surfaced late, weakly, repeatedly or not
  at all, and who were accountable for the resulting priority or action;
- 2 People/HR leaders for contextual workflow contrast only;
- 2 public/community/research commissioners for contextual workflow contrast
  only.

Before contact, create the complete eligible-account frame available under the
approved source boundary. Record recruitment source, prior founder/partner
relationship as `none`, `weak` or `strong`, contact attempts, refusals,
exclusions and completion. Recruit primary candidates consecutively against the
eligibility rule rather than selecting for responsiveness.

For each, reconstruct the latest real case: the pain point, who first knew,
when and how it surfaced, who needed to know, what blocked or distorted it,
current tools and meetings, disagreement, owner, priority/action,
follow-through, consequence, frequency, budget and switching/procurement
constraints. Do not present features until the behavioural account is complete.
At the end, primary candidates with the repeated job may be asked whether they
will receive a fixed-scope paid concierge proposal; agreement to receive a
proposal is not counted as payment.

Record only a de-identified evidence matrix in the engagement. Keep contact
details and raw notes in private storage outside Git, delete raw notes within 30
days of interview synthesis, and do not store employee names, issue text or
sensitive cases in the repository.

A survey, landing page or concept-preference question would be smaller in effort
but would not identify the actual workflow, buyer, consequence or budget path.

## Recruitment and Interview Protocol

### Primary eligibility

A primary participant must:

1. currently be, or have been within the previous 12 months, a development
   manager or product manager;
2. have owned the priority, response or follow-through for a material pain point
   that surfaced late, weakly, repeatedly or not at all in the previous 90 days;
3. be able to discuss the process without naming employees, customers,
   confidential projects or sensitive allegations; and
4. consent to a 30–40 minute research interview with the retention rule above.

### Invitation

> I am researching how development and product managers learn about important
> problems in time to act. I would like to discuss one recent real case—what
> happened, how it surfaced, what tools were used and what followed. This is a
> 30–40 minute research conversation, not a product demo. Please do not share
> employee names, confidential project details or sensitive allegations. Your
> name will not appear in the engagement evidence, and you may stop at any time.
> Would you be open to participating?

### Behavioural interview guide

Ask about the most recent eligible case before mentioning IssueNumber.one:

1. What was the pain point, and who first experienced or recognised it?
2. When and how did it reach you? Who needed to know earlier?
3. What delayed, weakened, filtered or distorted the signal?
4. Which meetings, chat, tickets, surveys, documents or informal routes were
   involved? What worked and failed?
5. What decision or action did the pain point affect, and what happened next?
6. What was the consequence of learning late or not acting?
7. How often does a materially similar failure occur?
8. Who owns the problem, decision and budget today?
9. What current alternative would have to be replaced or supplemented?
10. What outcome would justify paying to improve this process, and who would
    approve that spend?

Only after the behavioural account is complete, ask whether the participant
would accept a later fixed-scope paid proposal. Do not show features, name a
price, lead with the sponsor's diagnosis or count courtesy interest as demand.

### De-identified evidence matrix

Record participant code, role, organisation-size band, recruitment source,
relationship strength, eligible-case date band, failure pattern, current
alternative, consequence, recurrence, decision owner, economic buyer/budget
path, proposal-step response, withdrawal/missing state and researcher confidence.
Do not record employer, personal name, contact details or case text in Git.

## Owner, Cost and Duration

- **Owner:** Sponsor/founder for access; Managing Partner or named researcher
  for protocol and synthesis.
- **Participants:** 12 authorised professional participants as defined above.
- **Cost / budget source:** Up to 20–25 combined founder/research hours are
  authorised. No incentives or other spend are authorised.
- **Duration:** Three weeks from first authorised contact.
- **Stop conditions:** Stop immediately for confidentiality/safeguarding risk,
  participant withdrawal, requests for employee-identifying data, or evidence
  that the protocol is recruiting people who did not own the decision.

## Metrics and Guardrails

- **Success (exploratory gate):** At least 5 of 8 primary participants,
  including at least 2 development managers and 2 product managers,
  independently describe the same materially similar pain-point-surfacing and
  priority-to-action failure in a recent real case, say it recurs at least
  quarterly, and name a consequence and current alternative; at least 3 name
  the economic buyer/budget path; and at least 2 accept a concrete paid-
  concierge proposal step. At least 2 successful problem cases must come from
  accounts with no prior founder/partner relationship; otherwise the result is
  only founder-network feasibility. Report each role separately before any
  combined conclusion. Contextual interviews cannot support cross-segment
  ordering.
- **Failure:** At most 2 of 8 primary participants share a recurring job, no
  participant can name a material action/budget consequence, or current forms,
  meetings and work trackers are consistently adequate.
- **Inconclusive:** Three or four primary participants share the job; cases
  split into incompatible workflows; budget owner remains unclear; or access
  bias means the sample cannot test the candidate segment. A pattern confined
  to one primary role is segment-specific evidence, not combined-segment
  success, and may justify only the one bounded follow-up. Because one case
  changes 4/8 to 5/8, any one-case classification, withdrawal or missing-data
  change that flips the branch makes the result inconclusive.
- **Guardrails / ethics:** Informed participation and withdrawal; no deceptive
  research; no employee solicitation through managers; no raw personal or
  sensitive issue data in Git; no claims of representativeness; no incentives;
  outreach only under the accepted eligibility/invitation protocol; disclose
  the research purpose and any future sales transition.

## Expected Learning

The experiment can raise or lower confidence in the boundary-neutral job and
clarify buyer, current alternative, value language and payment path. O3/O4 are
not tested on relative demand. It does not validate O1/O2, a product, price,
market size or channel.

## Precommitted Decisions

- **On success:** Proceed to the synthetic mechanism/trust comparison and offer
  the paid concierge contract to qualifying primary candidates.
- **On failure:** Stop the priority-to-action direction; retain no-build and
  reopen the strongest evidenced comparator rather than broadening the platform
  claim.
- **If inconclusive:** Run no product build. Resolve the specific sample/job/
  buyer ambiguity with at most one narrower follow-up cohort and the same
  failure rules. If that remains inconclusive, park the direction for 90 days
  unless materially new evidence arrives.
