# IssueNumber.one Product Strategy Recommendation

## Decision Needed

The sponsor must decide whether product discovery should focus on one internal,
boundary-neutral priority-to-action journey while keeping the service,
standalone and Sneat Work forms unresolved, and keeping public research,
employee monitoring/organisational health, benchmarking and Moodometer
combination outside the initial promise.

The sponsor authorised buyer/problem discovery on 2026-08-04 with development
managers and product managers as the initial primary segment. This does not yet
accept a product boundary or build. See the
[sponsor decision](../decisions/sponsor-discovery-authorization.md).

This is a product-boundary recommendation, not an architecture, interface or
implementation design.

## Recommendation

Define the initial product hypothesis as a **bounded decision cycle**, not an
intelligence platform:

1. a development manager or product manager names a real product/delivery
   decision, participants and close date;
2. eligible contributors state few issues and allocate scarce support;
3. a decision owner sees the signal and its limitations;
4. the owner acknowledges, acts, defers, rejects or transfers each material
   issue with a reason;
5. every participant sees a safe terminal outcome;
6. the closed cycle can be replayed without exposing unnecessary identity or
   creating a permanent person/team trait.

Use relationship context only when one verified work relationship changes this
journey—for example declared team scope, accountable owner or explicit workflow
dependency. Do not make graph contribution a product success metric. Prefer
issue/action history over person-centred inferred relationships as the candidate
learning asset.

The initial promise excludes:

- population-representative public research or public-opinion claims;
- cross-organisation benchmark claims;
- organisational-health, performance, sentiment or wellbeing scoring;
- individual Moodometer joins;
- absolute anonymity while an identity link remains possible;
- raw employee free-text, individual votes or derived labels as reusable Sneat
  platform graph signals;
- automatic routing through a manager/relationship that may be the subject of
  the concern.

## End-to-End Journey and Observable Good Results

### Start — a bounded decision exists

The convenor states the decision, decision owner, eligible contributors,
attribution/confidentiality promise, what input can change, close date and
possible dispositions.

**Observable good result:** the record exists; contributor and decision owner
can both explain who sees what, what happens without participation and what
terminal states mean before anyone else acts.

### Contribution — nothing happens or a scarce signal forms

Each contributor can submit a bounded number of issues and allocate a bounded
support budget. Declining to submit remains distinct from disagreement.

**Observable good result:** contribution limits are applied consistently; the
participant sees the accepted state and audience; no response is recorded as
missing, not as “no issue.”

### Other actor response — the signal changes work

The authorised decision owner sees the ranked issues, scope and limitations and
records acknowledgement, action, deferral, rejection or safe transfer.

**Observable good result:** state advances and the **other actor** can act at the
necessary detail without using graph traversal to identify a contributor.

### Terminal outcome — every actor receives closure

The cycle closes with outcome, reason, accountable owner where applicable and
review/retention state.

**Observable good result:** contributors and decision owner see the terminal
state; the product does not present ranking as resolution or silence as health.

### Null-action branch

If nobody contributes, close as no/insufficient participation. If the owner does
nothing, preserve a dated awaiting-response or not-acted state rather than
fabricating closure.

**Observable good result:** participant silence and owner inaction remain
observable facts, not inferred agreement or organisational wellbeing.

### Close epilogue

The convenor ends collection; reminders stop; unresolved safeguarding or
external-process items transfer explicitly; retention promises take effect.

**Observable good result:** no provisional issue remains presented as an active
commitment and sensitive material does not survive by accident.

### Share/replay epilogue

An authorised successor receives the decision, instrument, participants/scope,
field dates, issue/action history, limitations and outcome—not identity-control
secrets or an employee free-text corpus.

**Observable good result:** another authorised actor can understand and repeat
the cycle without this engagement transcript or unnecessary personal data.

## Evidence and Assumptions

- **Evidence:** Both modes require an action loop; internal mode has employment
  power, re-identification and retaliation risks that make precise promises and
  safe recipients part of validity
  ([product/trust report](../research/product-modes-and-trust.md)).
- **Evidence:** No direct network effect, graph lift or universal safe signal
  reuse exists; explicit responsibility context and issue/action history are
  narrower candidates
  ([graph report](../research/graph-value-and-defensibility.md)).
- **Evidence:** The boundary-neutral job has the strongest relative bounded
  outcome and cold-start learning profile, not validated user demand
  ([strategic options](../research/strategic-options.md)).
- **Assumption:** Scarcity changes priority quality rather than merely causing
  frustration or suppressing minority evidence. The mechanism test can reject
  it.
- **Assumption:** A decision owner can provide a meaningful terminal response.
  If not, collection is not intelligence and the journey fails.
- **Confidence:** Low. No user has completed the journey and no action or repeat
  outcome exists.

## Options and Trade-offs

| Option | Benefits | Costs / risks | Reversibility | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Status quo Feature tree | Preserves current scarce issue/vote concept | No acceptance criteria or implemented journey; action/trust gaps remain | High | Current Conceptual canon |
| Standalone scarce-priority cycle | Clean test of scarcity without ecosystem dependence | Manual context/action; another destination/tool | High | Mechanic exists conceptually only |
| **Boundary-neutral bounded priority-to-action cycle** | One end-to-end job and accountable closure can be tested without product choice | Facilitation, scarcity and context effects remain confounded until tested | High | Recommended learning object, not product boundary |
| Sneat Work capability | May reuse verified work context | Could import Sneat Work adoption/trust assumptions; no necessary relationship outcome | High before implementation | Must not outrank standalone/service yet |
| Employee intelligence mode | Larger recurring scope and adjacent category budget | Monitoring, anonymity, suite competition and action-debt burden | Medium/low | Category practice, no wedge |
| Public research mode | Public participation and governed reporting possibilities | Several incompatible methods and trust models | Medium | Method evidence, no buyer/product outcome |
| One broad platform/two modes | Reuses concepts and brand | Context collapse, misunderstood reuse, scope before evidence | Low | Shared vocabulary only |

## Signal Boundaries

| Signal | Initial use | Cross-product posture | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Decision scope and declared owner | Route/interpret one cycle | Read explicit Sneat Work relationship if necessary; no inferred edge | Bounded beneficiary and non-graph fallback exist |
| Issue and scarce support | Prioritise within originating cycle | No individual vote export; no person-trait write | Minority/attribution harm and no external beneficiary |
| Disposition/action outcome | Close loop and support authorised replay | Deliberate handoff to a Sneat Work action may be tested | Outcome can be useful without exposing author |
| Anonymous-author/control mapping | Integrity and contributor control only | Isolated from managers, analytics, AI and shared graph | Direct re-identification key |
| Free text, mood, moderation/safeguarding evidence | Originating restricted purpose only | Isolated by default | Re-identification, retaliation, health and legal risk |
| Derived theme/health/performance label | Not in initial promise | No graph write | Unvalidated, contestable inference |
| Benchmark contribution | Not in initial promise | Isolated unless a future method/consortium is approved | Organisation identification, bias and permission risk |

## Dependencies and Sequence

1. Discover the buyer/problem before committing to this boundary.
2. Specify the synthetic journey and non-graph comparator.
3. Test scarcity, action and one explicit context independently.
4. Run a manual paid cycle before any product implementation and label it
   facilitated-service evidence.
5. Require at least two second paid cycles without founder facilitation before
   claiming product willingness to pay.
6. Record mechanism, paired-actor trust, terminal-state and delivery-cost
   results.
7. Only after repeat product evidence compare standalone versus Sneat Work with
   identical cycles and consider promoting requirements to owning Features.

## Verifies

- **Start isolation test:** both actors can predict recipients, attribution,
  purpose, null action and terminal states from the concept alone.
- **Contribution isolation test:** scarcity is enforced; missing response is not
  disagreement; no identity is exposed through role/scope context.
- **Other-actor isolation test:** the owner can choose a justified disposition
  without guessing the author; contributor later sees the state change.
- **Terminal isolation test:** all actors see a true end state; ranking alone
  fails; inaction stays visible.
- **Whole-journey test:** a future e2e surface must walk decision creation,
  contribution, another-actor response, terminal closure, null action, close and
  replay without manual database edits or hidden facilitator interpretation.

## Dissent and Unresolved Challenges

- The product may work equally well with ordinary explicit fields and no Sneat
  Work relationship context. That would weaken the integration/graph thesis but
  not necessarily the decision-cycle job.
- Scarcity may hide important minority/safeguarding issues. Some classes may
  need a separate non-ranked escalation path, which could fragment the promise.
- A generic work tracker plus structured meeting may deliver the same action
  outcome with lower switching cost.
- The standalone IssueNumber.one brand may produce clearer discovery than a
  capability inside Sneat Work; no entry-path evidence resolves this.
- A decision-owner-only concept test cannot validate contributor trust. The
  revised experiment uses paired contributor/owner roles and zero unsafe
  identity guesses as a gate.
- The independent [Devil's Advocate review](devils-advocate-review.md) retains
  its full dissent. Managing Partner dispositions are recorded in
  [recommendation resolution](../decisions/recommendation-resolution.md).

## Priority and Next Decision

The product priority is to prove or reject the end-to-end action journey and its
mechanisms before specifying a platform, public mode, graph asset or benchmark.

After the paid/repeat evidence, the sponsor decides among: stop; retain a
facilitated service; test a standalone scarce-priority tool against a Sneat Work
capability; or reopen a different mode from the strategic-options set.
