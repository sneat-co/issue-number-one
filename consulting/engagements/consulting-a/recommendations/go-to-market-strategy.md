# IssueNumber.one Go-to-Market Strategy Recommendation

## Decision Needed

The sponsor must decide whether initial go-to-market learning should use
founder-led problem discovery and a paid concierge decision cycle, with LinkedIn
as a measured access/content route rather than the primary platform or assumed
channel.

No outreach, campaign, paid media, account contact or spend is authorised by
this Draft recommendation.

## Recommendation

Do not launch broad awareness, paid LinkedIn acquisition, benchmark content or
a free-product growth loop.

Use a sequenced founder-led motion:

1. identify candidate leaders who recently owned a contested cross-functional
   priority decision;
2. discover the actual workflow, consequence, current alternative, decision
   owner and budget path;
3. offer a paid, manually delivered priority-to-action cycle only where the
   repeated job is present;
4. require an observable decision/action and a repeat condition;
5. only after one buyer/offer converts, compare direct trusted access with
   LinkedIn organic/targeted access using the same qualification and economic
   stages.

Position the outcome in customer language:

> Turn scattered competing issues into one accountable priority decision—and
> show contributors what happened next.

Do not lead with “relationship graph,” “AI issue intelligence,” “organisational
health,” “anonymous employee listening,” “public opinion” or “network effect.”
Those claims are either implementation language, separate jobs or unsupported.

## Initial Segment and Buying Hypothesis

The initial **candidate**, not validated segment, is a bounded product,
engineering, operations or departmental leadership group where:

- one leader owns a real cross-functional decision;
- issues arrive through meetings, chat, forms, tickets or escalation;
- the current process produces contested priority, repetition or no visible
  follow-through;
- contributors and the owner can both observe the terminal result;
- a budget path exists for improving the decision cycle.

Employees, community participants and the public may contribute in other modes,
but they are not the initial economic buyer hypothesis. People/HR and public
commissioners remain explicit comparator segments during discovery rather than
being silently treated as the same market.

## Commercial Hypothesis

- **Outcome sold:** one bounded priority-to-action decision cycle with a visible
  owner response and close.
- **Candidate payer:** leader accountable for the decision or the organisational
  budget owner supporting that function.
- **Candidate value metric:** accountable organisation/team scope or active
  decision cycle—not every contributor seat, vote or graph edge.
- **Initial proof:** paid concierge engagement, not waitlist selection or verbal
  willingness.
- **Pricing posture:** discover price through a paid offer tied to consequence
  and delivery cost; do not preserve the current landing tiers as validated.
- **Renewal posture:** repeat cycle, expanded accountable scope or explicitly
  stated renewal condition; participation alone is not retention.

No price point is recommended because adjacent list prices demonstrate category
packaging rather than willingness to pay for this outcome.

## Evidence and Assumptions

- **Evidence:** LinkedIn supports targeting, lead forms and later conversion
  measurement but has no IssueNumber.one buyer, CPL, qualification, sales-cycle
  or revenue evidence
  ([growth/commercial report](../research/growth-and-commercial-evidence.md)).
- **Evidence:** Direct sales, partners, communities, content, invitations and no
  launch are credible comparators; product-led recipient value is unproven
  ([growth/commercial report](../research/growth-and-commercial-evidence.md#channel-and-loop-comparison)).
- **Evidence:** Current landing prices and plan selections are hypotheses with
  no inspected traffic/conversion/payment outcome
  ([current-state baseline](../research/current-state-and-demand-evidence.md)).
- **Evidence:** Adjacent categories have real spend but not a transferable buyer
  or price for this job
  ([customer/market report](../research/customer-market-evidence.md)).
- **Assumption:** The founder can obtain authorised access to relevant leaders
  through existing relationships or selective professional outreach. If false,
  trusted partner/community access may be necessary.
- **Assumption:** A concierge delivery can expose the job and willingness to pay
  without product construction. If false, define the smallest concept/prototype
  needed rather than building a platform.
- **Confidence:** Low. The unknown most likely to change the recommendation is a
  named buyer who pays and repeats.

## Options and Trade-offs

| Motion | Benefits | Costs / risks | Reversibility | Current evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Status quo/no external action | No spend or reputational risk | No buyer/channel/payment learning | Highest | Safe default |
| **Founder-led discovery + paid concierge** | Highest context per account; can test problem, buyer, payment and action without product | Founder time; selection bias; outreach/retention governance needed | High | Best decision yield, not proven scale |
| LinkedIn organic/targeted access | Professional reach, thought leadership and measurable links | Audience fit is not economics; content can attract peers rather than buyers | High | Platform capability only |
| LinkedIn paid lead generation | Scalable targeting and instrumentation | Premature spend; raw leads/waitlist can hide poor qualification and revenue | High financially, reputational cost possible | No CAC/offer evidence |
| Partners/consultants/associations | Borrowed trust and client/member context | Incentive, enablement and partner economics unknown | Medium/high | Plausible, not evidenced |
| Professional communities | Access to repeated practitioner problems | Permission/moderation burden; attention is not budget | High | Plausible, not evidenced |
| Benchmark/content report | Decision artifact and shareability | Method/evidence production burden; H4/H7 unresolved | Medium | Premature |
| Product-led invitations | Potential low-cost recipient loop | No product, activation, immediate recipient value or re-invitation evidence | Medium | Hypothesis only |

## Funnel and Economic Measurement

Use one funnel across every motion:

1. **eligible account** — matches the defined role, decision and context;
2. **qualified problem** — recent recurring consequential workflow and failed
   alternative are evidenced;
3. **economic buyer/buying group** — owner, budget path and approval actors are
   named;
4. **paid cycle** — money or contracted commitment, not plan selection;
5. **activation to outcome** — decision occurs and the other actor sees the
   response/terminal state;
6. **repeat/retention** — another cycle, expanded scope or explicit renewal;
7. **fully loaded economics** — media, founder time, partner cost, research,
   facilitation, support and measurement against retained revenue.

Impressions, followers, downloads, raw leads, waitlist signups, contributions,
invitations and issue counts are diagnostic only.

## LinkedIn Role

LinkedIn should be treated as three separable hypotheses:

- **research access:** identify and, if authorised, contact candidate decision
  owners;
- **credibility/content distribution:** share a decision-useful artifact after
  real evidence exists;
- **paid acquisition:** buy measurable reach only after buyer, offer,
  qualification and retained-value stages are stable.

LinkedIn must not become the system of record, product identity or sole source
of relationships. Any channel comparison should preserve account, role, offer,
stage date, source, cost and retained outcome; attribution is not incrementality.

## Dependencies and Sequence

1. Sponsor supplies/declines non-identifying current demand evidence.
2. Sponsor approves participant, outreach, retention and evidence boundaries for
   discovery.
3. Complete buyer/problem discovery and stop if thresholds fail.
4. Complete mechanism/trust test before implying product capability.
5. Offer paid concierge cycles; record decision outcome and repeat condition.
6. If an offer converts, run the controlled channel comparison.
7. Only then decide positioning, price metric, LinkedIn investment, partner
   motion or product-led loop.

## Dissent and Unresolved Challenges

- Founder-led discovery may overfit warm relationships and founder persuasion;
  the experiment includes comparable qualification and explicit failure states.
- Requiring early payment may reject a valuable job whose procurement cycle is
  longer than the experiment. Contracted budget-owner commitment can be
  recorded separately from compliments or waitlist intent.
- LinkedIn organic activity may be useful before paid evidence, but content
  output can become vanity work. Every artifact needs a named decision user and
  downstream qualified stage.
- A partner/community route may outperform founder access where trust is domain-
  specific. The channel experiment must allow LinkedIn to lose.
- The existing landing may confuse results because it tests older positioning;
  do not interpret its aggregate as demand for the recommended promise.
- Independent Devil's Advocate review is pending.

## Priority and Next Decision

The GTM priority is proof of a buyer/problem/payment chain, not reach.

After a paid repeated outcome exists, decide whether LinkedIn, direct founder
access, a partner, a community or another route has the best fully loaded path
to retained qualified accounts. Until then, LinkedIn remains a candidate
instrument—not the strategy.
