# IssueNumber.one Sponsor Discovery Authorization

## Question and Owner

- Question: Does the sponsor accept the boundary-neutral learning direction and
  authorise the first buyer/problem discovery contract?
- Decision owner: Sponsor.
- Deadline: Answered 2026-08-04.

## Context

The sponsor confirmed that no website, waitlist, usage, payment or retention
evidence is available. The sponsor also supplied firsthand experience from work
as a development manager and across multiple corporations: important pain
points often fail to bubble up, the problem is not addressed adequately, and
the sponsor believes IssueNumber.one can address it.

This is relevant founder evidence for problem plausibility. It is not counted
as independent customer evidence, demand validation, product use or willingness
to pay.

## Options

| Option | Evidence | Trade-offs | Risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stop/no-build | No external demand evidence exists | Avoids research time | Leaves the founder-observed problem untested | Highest |
| Investigate the prior broad leader segment | Reversible but diffuse | Wider coverage | Weak job/role comparability | High |
| Investigate development and product managers | Direct sponsor experience and a more coherent operational context | Still unvalidated and founder-shaped | Warm-network and confirmation bias | High |

## Criteria and Assumptions

- Decision criteria: repeated recent behaviour, named consequence, inadequate
  current alternative, economic buyer/budget path, non-warm evidence and a safe
  bounded research protocol.
- Assumptions: the sponsor's “we can try” response accepts the proposed research
  defaults: 12 interviews, no incentives, private raw notes outside Git,
  de-identified engagement evidence and deletion of raw notes after 30 days.
  If the sponsor corrects this interpretation, the affected activity stops and
  this record is superseded.

## Dissent

- Founder experience can improve the problem statement while increasing
  confirmation and warm-network bias. It does not satisfy any experiment
  success threshold.
- “Pain points do not bubble up” may be an issue-surfacing problem, a management
  behaviour problem, an action/closure problem or several incompatible jobs.
  Discovery must allow those explanations to separate.
- Development managers and product managers may have different buyers,
  workflows and incentives. Results must be reported by role before combining.

## Decision and Rationale

1. **Existing evidence inventory:** Recorded as unavailable. Traction remains
   unknown; it is not treated as zero or positive.
2. **Learning direction:** Accepted with an amendment. The initial primary
   segment is development managers and product managers, not generic team
   leaders.
3. **Discovery:** Authorised under the defaults above for up to 20–25 combined
   founder/research hours. No incentives are authorised. Outreach is limited to
   the accepted eligibility rule and invitation; participation remains
   voluntary and withdrawals are honoured.
4. **Authority boundary:** This does not approve a product, a facilitated paid
   offer, a Sneat Work or standalone boundary, later experiments, a campaign,
   architecture, implementation or canonical promotion.
