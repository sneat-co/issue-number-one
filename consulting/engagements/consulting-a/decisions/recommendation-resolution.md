# IssueNumber.one Recommendation Challenge Resolution

## Question and Owner

- Question: Which recommendation claims survive the independent challenge, and
  what must change before the recommendation set can enter sponsor review?
- Decision owner: Managing Partner for synthesis quality; sponsor for any later
  strategic direction, outreach, spend or product commitment.
- Deadline: Before Executive Editor synthesis and sponsor review.

## Context

The independent [Devil's Advocate review](../recommendations/devils-advocate-review.md)
found that the engagement's negative conclusions were well supported, but the
positive ranking of a Sneat Work implementation was not. It identified four
blocking and ten additional challenges. This decision resolves those challenges
without converting absent customer evidence into recommendation confidence.

## Options

| Option | Evidence | Trade-offs | Risks | Reversibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retain the original O2/Sneat Work ordering | Ecosystem fit and possible reuse only | Simple story | Confuses internal fit with buyer value | Medium |
| Reject all positive learning directions | Strong absence of demand evidence | Maximum restraint | Discards a cheap, testable job hypothesis | High |
| Narrow to a boundary-neutral learning candidate | Adversarial review permits research into the priority-to-action job | Preserves uncertainty and requires staged evidence | May still end in no product | High |

## Criteria and Assumptions

- Decision criteria: evidence traceability, discrimination among live options,
  explicit trust boundaries, precommitted failure states, reversibility and no
  unsupported ecosystem preference.
- Assumptions: no undisclosed customer, usage, payment, repeat or comparative
  mechanism evidence is available; sponsor-supplied evidence may later change
  the baseline but may not be presumed.

## Dissent

The seven retained dissent positions in the [independent review](../recommendations/devils-advocate-review.md#retained-dissent)
remain live. In particular, no-build may be the best decision; service,
standalone and Sneat Work remain equivalent on present evidence; O3/O4 are not
disproved on demand; early payment may prove founder-led consulting only; and a
bounded operational success provides no evidence for public research,
organisational health, Moodometer integration, benchmarking or graph
compounding.

## Decision and Rationale

The broad relationship-aware issue-intelligence platform is **not** recommended
on current evidence. A bounded priority-to-action job is retained only as a
boundary-neutral learning candidate. The implementation boundary remains
explicitly unresolved among facilitated service, standalone workflow and a
Sneat Work capability. Public research, employee listening, benchmarking,
organisational health, Moodometer integration and graph-defensibility claims
remain outside the learning envelope.

The revised recommendation set may enter sponsor review because every blocker
has been removed as a claim or resolved in the experiment contract. This is not
sponsor acceptance, experiment authorisation or permission to build.
