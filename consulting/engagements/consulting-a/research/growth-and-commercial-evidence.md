# IssuerNumber.One Growth and Commercial Evidence

## Owned Question

Who plausibly pays for which outcome and value metric, what adjacent-category
pricing/commercial evidence exists, and which acquisition/distribution motions
could economically reach an initial buyer? This evaluates H5 and H7, plus the
commercial implication of H4, without choosing a strategy.

## Concise Answer

There are plausible category buyers, but no evidence identifies an
IssuerNumber.one initial buyer or proves willingness to pay. The current
scarce-priority mechanism most directly maps to a departmental leader
accountable for resolving contested priorities; product, engineering and
operations leaders are candidate economic buyers. A peer-comparison/benchmark
offer would instead require a buyer accountable for organisation or people
decisions (for example People/HR, operations, or an executive sponsor), but H7
is unvalidated because no credible peer definition, methodology, decision use,
or willingness-to-pay evidence exists.

Adjacent vendors package feedback/research workflows using different exposure
metrics: tracked feedback users, product-maker seats, and survey seats plus
response volume. That shows category packaging conventions, **not** an
IssuerNumber.one price point, buyer, value metric, or willingness to pay.

LinkedIn has official targeting, lead-form and conversion-measurement
capabilities, so it is a measurable paid/organic professional-distribution
candidate. It is not economically evidenced: no audience access, offer,
creative, cost, qualified-account, sales-cycle, or retained-revenue baseline
exists. Direct founder sales, partnerships, professional communities,
content/benchmark reports, product-led invitations and status quo/no launch
remain material alternatives.

- Read the approved engagement brief/checkpoint and inspected the current

## Findings

### Observed or sourced

1. **The current offer mixes commercial units without evidence of the matching
   buyer.** It exposes team, lifetime, company and corporate packaging (E2),
   while the product core is a scarce-priority mechanism (E1). The seed proposes
   a departmental-leadership buyer and free-to-organisation model (E11), but no
   source shows which unit a buyer would fund.
2. **Adjacent categories charge the party administering, analysing, or receiving
   scaled exposure—not necessarily every participant.** Canny uses tracked
   feedback users (E3), Productboard makers (E4), and SurveyMonkey creator
   seats plus response capacity (E5). These are packaging precedents only.
3. **Benchmarking is commercially unevidenced.** The current product supplies
   priority aggregation but no peer cohort, sampling, comparability, consent,
   benchmark methodology or decision result (E1). No source found demonstrates
   organisations would pay IssuerNumber.one to understand themselves relative
   to peers.
4. **LinkedIn is instrumentable, not proven economical.** Lead forms expose CPL
   and form completion (E6); conversion tracking can ingest later/offline
   outcomes (E7). No current evidence supplies qualified-account cost, meeting
   rate, sales cycle, win rate or retained revenue.
5. **B2B buying may need group consensus.** Gartner supports treating
   multi-function buying groups as a measurement concern (E8). 6sense supports
   the narrower caution that buyers may form a shortlist before sales contact
   (E9), but its sample must not be projected onto a small-team product.

### Inferred

1. **Payer/outcome mappings need separate validation.** From E1, E2 and E11,
   the closest mapping is a product, engineering, operations or department
   leader paying for a trusted, actionable top-priority signal. Candidate value
   metrics are accountable team/organisation scope or active decision cycles,
   with administrator exposure secondary. This can fail if contributors use the
   product but leaders do not change a decision or fund continuation.
2. **H7 can only be tested after peer comparison is a credible decision input.**
   It needs a cohort, common measure, permitted aggregation and a decision that
   changes with the comparison. A benchmark price or network-effect claim before
   then would confuse planned data with commercial value.
3. **H5 cannot be settled by professional audience fit.** LinkedIn could reach a
   defined buyer, but direct founder sales, a credible partner, a community,
   decision-useful content, or a recipient-value invitation can beat it on
   qualified-account cost. Each can also fail through poor access or trust.

## Funnel, Value and Measurement Rules

1. **Use economic stages:** eligible account → qualified account/problem →
   buying-group contact or decision conversation → funded purchase → activation
   to promised outcome → renewal/expansion. Impressions, followers, raw
   waitlist signups, downloads and invitations sent are diagnostic inputs only.
2. **Measure the promised outcome separately from acquisition.** For scarce
   priority: baseline decision-cycle/escalation friction, participation,
   percentage of top issues with documented owner/action, and buyer-validated
   usefulness. For a benchmark: cohort eligibility, method completion, decision
   use and renewal—not data volume.
3. **Calculate fully loaded economics only when facts exist.** Include media,
   founder/sales time, partner compensation, content/research production,
   methodology, onboarding, support and measurement. Revenue must be collected
   or contracted and retained, not a listed plan or stated intent.
4. **Avoid false attribution.** LinkedIn can connect campaign activity to
   conversions (E7), but does not prove incrementality itself. Preserve source,
   account, offer, buying-group role, stage date and outcome across channels.

## Contradictions and Disconfirming Evidence

- Listed suite prices (E2) must remain offer hypotheses; treating them as
  willingness-to-pay evidence contradicts the engagement evidence rule.
- The founder's public-leaderboard loop (E11) lacks evidence that a public-topic
  participant gains value that causes team adoption, or that public/internal
  trust domains share a commercial path.
- LinkedIn documentation favours its platform. It is useful for instrumentation,
  not independent proof of IssuerNumber.one CAC, ROAS or audience fit.
- The 6sense high-spend sample (E9) cannot estimate this product's sales cycle,
  buyer count or channel return. Gartner supports caution, not segment choice.

## Confidence

**Low-medium overall.** Current first-party pages support the adjacent pricing
and LinkedIn-capability facts. Confidence is low on the decision that matters:
who pays, for what, at what price and through which motion. The unknown most
likely to change the result is a named, repeated, budget-backed problem for a
defined initial segment.

## Risks, Dependencies and Recommended Research Priority

1. **First, establish buyer/problem/outcome evidence before price/channel
   conclusions.** The smallest safe action is the engagement brief's approved
   request for sponsor-provided, non-identifying aggregate evidence: landing
   traffic, monthly unique waitlist totals, selected-plan/source totals if
   captured, and de-identified prior problem-conversation summaries. No raw
   emails or private notes. Absence must remain absence, not validation.
2. **Second, define a benchmark validity gate, not a benchmark offer.** Name the
   decision a peer comparison changes, eligible population, comparator, minimum
   cohort/method, permitted data use and reason more participation improves it.
3. **Third, make later channel comparisons falsifiable.** Define one buyer, one
   problem, one offer and shared qualification/opportunity/revenue/cost
   definitions across every motion. This is measurement design only, not a
   campaign or outreach proposal.

Dependencies: sponsor authorization is required for private aggregate evidence,
outreach, paid media, partner/community contact, customer research, campaign
execution or canonical promotion. Benchmarking additionally depends on
product/trust research resolving consent, anonymity, representativeness and
data-rights constraints.

## Smallest Learning Actions

- **Now:** preserve this public/repository baseline and record the absence of
  observed buyer, cost and conversion evidence.
- **Sponsor-owned:** provide or explicitly decline the non-identifying aggregate
  evidence inventory above; this distinguishes an unmeasured probe from no
  available demand signal without exposing personal data.
- **Later, only with explicit authorization:** compare one buyer/problem/offer
  across at least two motions plus status quo, using predeclared qualified
  account, funded purchase and early outcome/retention thresholds.

## Alternatives

- Retain the scarce-priority product as a standalone internal workflow and make
  no benchmark, public-research or network-effect premium claim.
- Treat public research, internal organisation intelligence and product feedback
  as separate commercial questions rather than one price ladder.
- Do not launch/spend externally until buyer/problem and demand evidence exists.
- Use a non-LinkedIn route if evidence later shows superior trusted access or
  recipient value; professional audience fit alone is insufficient.

## Proposed Experiments

No experiment is proposed for execution: this iteration prohibits outreach,
campaigns, private analytics and external action. If separately authorized, the
smallest decision-changing test is a controlled comparison of a single defined
buyer/problem/offer against status quo and one alternative route, measured from
qualified account through funded purchase and early outcome/retention. Sponsor
approval must first set owner, budget, consent, duration, data retention and
success/failure/inconclusive thresholds.

## Dissent and Conflicts Requiring Resolution

- Landing prices and founder monetisation hypotheses may look decisive, but they
  are not willingness-to-pay evidence.
- LinkedIn capability claims do not choose LinkedIn; later comparison may favour
  direct sales, partners, communities, content, invitations, or no launch.
- A benchmark can sound defensible while participation lacks validity and
  permission. H4/H7 need research and trust evidence before synthesis.
